Andar Studio Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

Andar Studio respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle
information when you use our games and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information needed to provide our services, including: -
Account information (if used) - Player name or username - Game progress
and settings - Technical information for improving games

How We Use Information

We use information to: - Provide game features - Save player progress -
Improve performance - Fix bugs - Provide support

Information Sharing

Andar Studio does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services such as cloud services,
authentication, analytics, or advertising if enabled.

Children’s Privacy

We aim to create safe experiences for players and do not intentionally
collect sensitive personal information from children.
